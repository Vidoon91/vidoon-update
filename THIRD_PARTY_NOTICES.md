## BgUtils PO Token Provider

Vidoon can bundle BgUtils PO Token Provider 1.3.2 from
https://github.com/Brainicism/bgutil-ytdlp-pot-provider.

The provider is licensed under GPL-3.0-only. Its source, license, upstream
README, and Vidoon modification notice are included in the prepared
`vendor/bgutil-provider` directory. Vidoon changes the provider server binding
to `127.0.0.1` so it is not exposed to other computers.
